Eun-Seo Han

Transportation Planning · Public Transportation · Multimodal Mobility

I am a transportation researcher interested in data-driven approaches to understanding urban mobility and improving public transportation systems.

My research focuses on public transportation planning, multimodal mobility, transportation accessibility, and mobility data analytics, with particular interest in connecting quantitative analysis with practical transportation planning and policy.
