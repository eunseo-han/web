Projects

Jul 2025 – Present
Feasibility Reassessment and Development Strategy for Seoul Urban Railway Network Plan
Funded by Seoul Metropolitan Government
  • Analyze travel demand patterns, network connectivity, and accessibility impacts to assess feasibility of proposed and petitioned rail lines.
  • Identify priority corridors by incorporating inputs from district governments and public stakeholders.
  • Design a Seoul Urban Railway Network Plan aligned with travel behavior and policy goals, reducing underserved rail service areas from 37% to 27%.
Jan 2025 – Jun 2025
Sustainable Urban Transport System for Carbon Neutrality in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan
Funded by Korea International Cooperation Agency (KOICA)
  • Evaluated urban transport conditions using multi-source data (traffic demand, network structure, field-based data) to diagnose inefficiencies in Bishkek’s mobility system.
  • Formulated mid- to long-term sustainable transport strategies integrating public transit enhancement and carbon reduction measures, proposing 190 initiatives across six strategic sectors.
Apr 2024 – Dec 2024
Development of Integrated Multimodal Mobility Service and Evaluation Platform
Funded by National Research Foundation of Korea
  • Developed an integrated multimodal mobility platform with demand forecast and optimization models to support service planning and social benefit evaluation.
  • Integrated a mobility service framework by linking data infrastructure with operational processes and analyzing first- and last-mile connectivity using smart card and bike-sharing data.
Jun 2022 – Dec 2024
Revision of the Korea Highway Capacity Manual (KHCM)
Funded by Korea Transport Institute (KOTI)
  • Revised KHCM to incorporate emerging mobility modes, evolving roadway system characteristics, and advanced traffic analysis methodologies affecting capacity and level of service.
  • Developed and validated a conflict-based methodology using Surrogate Safety Measure (SSM), demonstrating that ramp influence areas extend beyond conventional speed-based definitions (~50 m upstream).
Dec 2023 – Jun 2024
Study on Transfer Fare Integration and Periodic Revenue Clearing Mechanisms
Funded by Metropolitan Transport Commission, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT)
  • Established a transparent and rational transfer fare clearing framework, including revised settlement rules, periodic clearing mechanisms, and inter-agency agreement schemes among metropolitan rail operators.
  • Coordinated with 11 operators to align stakeholder interests and drive consensus on fare settlement mechanisms.
